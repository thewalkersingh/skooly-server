🔑 Relationship Notes
School → Classroom: One school has many classrooms (@OneToMany).

Classroom → Section: One classroom has many sections.

Section → Subject: One section has many subjects.

Subject ↔ Teacher: Many‑to‑many (teachers can teach multiple subjects, subjects can be taught by multiple teachers).

Classroom → Student: One classroom has many students.

Section → Student: One section has many students.

Parent → Student: One parent can have many students (children).

Attendance → Student: Each attendance record belongs to one student.

Attendance → Teacher: Each attendance record is marked by one teacher.
